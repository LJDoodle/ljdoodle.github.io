Prism Sonnet (June 7, 2023)

Shall I compare thee to a summer's light,
Which struck upon the surface of a glass,
And rather than maintaining course in flight,
Makes wonderous shades to give a girl delight.

When often hours call for strength and head
You'd best forget me and maintain your course
But if you let me work with you instead,
My heart to joy by your hand shall be led

In years gone by I plucked a shard of glass
and thinking it might sparkle in the light
I held it so Apollo's rays could pass
So colors entertained me as a lass.

But like light’s awe, Ourania's vibrant force,
My sight, love's laws, together well perforce.

My Field (Sep. 29, 2019)

I gaze upon this tract I hath,
Two quarters barley, oats one-half,
And scarecrows spacéd down the way,
To dar’st not give the crows last laugh.

I pray that sweet Demeter green
Will bless this crop, and make it sheen,
‘Til fall doth rear its bitter head,
And harvest reap’d, and harvest glean’d

terror (July 28

i am afraid;
terrified that this unstoppable force
will meet no immovable object to stop it,
that this weight of this load will grow
until my neck fractures under its burden.

will the earth know
that this bundle of hope and desire
was a maze in its bliss?
that its love was of such an insatiable temper
it absumed me and made me small,
a torture of my body's own making.
